==============================================================================
  HOW TO READ IT
==============================================================================
  'pool<k' is the fraction of GTs holding fewer anchor centres than the
  budget. For those GTs every candidate is already a positive, so the
  RANKING cannot change the assignment and tal_beta's selection term is
  inert. Only its target-magnitude term acts on them.

  IF small is NOT binding (>50%):
    - raising tal_topk cannot help small objects. Confirmed independently
      by the footprint diagnostic: they take 7.73 against a budget of 10.
    - the beta plateau has a mechanical explanation, which is a stronger
      paper claim than the plateau on its own.
    - LOWERING topk is the only version of this experiment that can bite,
      and it must be judged on mAP50_small, not mAP50-95.

  IF small IS binding (<10%):
    - the saturation story is wrong; 7.73 < 10 is then the metric>eps
      prune, not geometry, and the beta plateau needs another explanation.

  CROSS-CHECK: 'take~' is the mean positives per GT ignoring the eps prune.
  Against the footprint diagnostic's stock column (small 7.73 / med 9.87 /
  large 9.79, taxonomy A, YOLOv12) 'take~' should be an UPPER BOUND. If it
  comes out BELOW 7.73 the geometry here disagrees with that run and one
  of the two is wrong — do not proceed to GPU until that is resolved.
